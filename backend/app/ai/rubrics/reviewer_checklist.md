# Reviewer Checklist

Review only the supplied candidate outputs and original evidence context. Do
not research new facts or invent issues.

- Factual claims must be supported by supplied evidence.
- Citation URLs must correspond to a URL in the original evidence context,
  allowing harmless normalization such as a trailing slash.
- Hypotheses and inferences must be explicitly labeled as inference.
- Opportunity-score reasons and the contact recommendation must explain why the
  cited evidence matters.
- The opportunity score and contact recommendation must be consistent with the
  supplied opportunity rubric.
- Weak or incomplete evidence must be reflected in caveats.
- Outreach must not claim an unproven problem, buying intent, budget, or planned
  purchase.
- Decision-maker suggestions must be role hypotheses, not invented named people.

Approve only when there are no material issues. Otherwise reject and return
clear revision instructions identifying the unsupported claim, missing citation,
or certainty problem.
